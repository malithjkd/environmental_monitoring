Key Findings

60W Avg Effective Power (of 800W)
6.5% Feedforward Duty (steady-state)
18.5% Optimal Duty (1 deg C/min ramp)
3e-08 Current ki Value
Root Cause: The feedforward computes steady-state maintenance power (6.5% = 52W), not ramp-up power. The PID ki=3e-08 is so small that even after 108 minutes of sustained error, the integral term contributes less than 1% duty. Effectively only ~60W of 800W is used during PASTEURIZE.

Recommendation: A ki value in the range 1e-04 to 5e-04 would significantly increase duty during PASTEURIZE while still allowing the integral to settle once the setpoint is reached. Combined with the existing anti-windup clamp, this should safely reduce PASTEURIZE time from ~96 min to an estimated 15-30 min.

RECOMMENDED: ki = 1e-04 — Good balance between speed and overshoot control. The anti-windup clamp (max integral = 1/ki = 10,000) limits the I-term to a maximum of 1.0 (100% duty), and the safety_max_temp cutoff provides a hard backstop.
